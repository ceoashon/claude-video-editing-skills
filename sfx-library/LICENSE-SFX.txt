DIGITAL EMPIRE SFX PACK
234 sound effects for short-form and YouTube edits
All files: WAV, 48kHz, 16-bit stereo, peak-normalised to -1 dB

FOLDERS
01 Whooshes & Transitions - jump cuts, zoom-ins, scene changes
02 Impacts & Booms        - reveals, hook lines, text slams
03 Risers & Tension       - the seconds before a reveal
04 Pops & Clicks          - captions, emojis, list items, screen recordings
05 Dings & Notifications  - right answers, DMs, wins, mistakes
06 Money                  - revenue, sales, client wins
07 Tech & Glitch          - AI/tech content, pattern breaks
08 Camera & Paper         - freeze frames, notes, page turns
09 Funny & Reactions      - comedic moments
10 Time                   - urgency, deadlines

LICENCE
You can use every sound in this pack in any video, personal or commercial,
on any platform (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, ads, client work).
No credit is required.
