# Interview + shot sheet

Your job is to find the story before anything gets recorded. Talking-head videos live or die on the story and the proof, not the editing.

## 1. Interview

Ask questions in rounds of 5–7, short enough to answer by voice-to-text. Ramble-y answers are fine; you're mining for details.

Round 1 covers the basics:
- **Before:** how did they do this before? How long did it take, what did it cost?
- **The switch:** what was the moment they changed? (This is usually the hook.)
- **Now:** real numbers. Time, money, results.
- **Worst moment:** what went wrong, what almost made them quit?
- **Wow moment:** the first time it actually worked.
- **Limits:** what still needs a human? Admitting limits makes the rest believable.
- **Viewer:** who is this for, and what will they want to know?

Round 2 is follow-ups on whatever has the best story. Chase:
- **Proof:** screenshots, receipts, archive photos, numbers on screen
- **Specifics:** "about 10 hours" beats "a long time"
- **Contradictions:** if two answers disagree, ask. Never guess a number.

Never invent numbers, features, quotes or results. If a fact is missing, leave a `[blank]` for them to say out loud.

## 2. Title first

Pick the title before the shot sheet. Formats that work for "here's what I learned" videos:
- `If I Started [X] in [year], I'd Do This`
- `I [did hard thing]. Here's What Happened`
- `[Big number] [unit] → [small number] (my exact setup)`

Only use a format the creator has proof for.

## 3. Shot sheet

Bullet points, not a script. One numbered take per idea, 1–2 bullets each, so every take is 1–2 minutes. The editor can rearrange takes later, so order matters less than coverage.

```
1. HOOK
- "Everything in this video was ..." (the result first)
- "If I started ..., here's exactly what I'd do."

2. WHERE I STARTED
- old way + real number

3. STEP 1: ...
...

N. OUTRO
- callback to the title + what's next
```

At the bottom, list the B-roll to screen-record today (apps, uploads, before/after).

Keep lines short: they'll read it off a second phone mid-take.
