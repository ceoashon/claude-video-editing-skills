# YouTube metadata

Work from the final cut, not the plan: read the transcript and the cut list so every claim matches what's actually said.

## Title
- One main title plus 2 backups for A/B testing.
- Under ~60 characters, with the strongest word early.
- The title and thumbnail should add to each other, not repeat. If the thumbnail says "10 HRS → 1 HR", the title shouldn't.
- No claim the video doesn't back up.

## Description
1. **First two lines** (shown above "more"): what the video is plus the payoff. Put links people were promised in the video here.
2. What's inside / resources.
3. **Chapters**, computed from the real edit timeline (`0:00` first, at least 3, each ≥ 10 s). Name them like a viewer would search.
4. Gear or tools mentioned, with the real prices said in the video.
5. A short sign-off or CTA in the creator's own voice.

## Tags
10–20, most specific first: the topic, the tools named, "how to" phrasings, the channel name.

## Pinned comment
The promised link plus one question that invites replies.

## Checks before posting
- Every number matches the video.
- Every link promised in the video is filled in. Never ship a `[placeholder]`.
- Flag anything that could read as contradictory (e.g. two different time claims) and suggest how to answer it.
